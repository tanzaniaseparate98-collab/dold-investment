FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I register?

Use the registration form with your valid email address and complete additional profile steps once the backend is connected.

Can I browse jobs on my phone?

Yes. The interface is mobile-first and designed for Android devices.

Are wallets active in this phase?

No. Wallet and transaction features are designed for Phase 4 and are not connected to real money in this phase.

Is investment active?

No. Investment-related services remain disabled until legal and regulatory review is completed.
